Hi, I’m Tom 👋

Over the past 13 years, I’ve worked across financial services, consulting, education, and nonprofits, designing products and helping the teams behind them do better work.

My background spans graphic design, front-end development, research, and product strategy, so I’m as interested in the details of an interface as I am in the bigger systems that shape it.

At heart, I’m a people person who thinks like a builder. I believe good design goes beyond what users see on a screen. It’s also about how teams work together, how stories get told, how decisions get made, and how ideas become real.

Today, I lead product design at Northwestern Mutual, where my team shapes the tools financial advisors use to manage their clients and businesses. I also teach UX/UI design at the University of Pennsylvania. I'm based in Brooklyn and live with my partner Mike and our black cat Mowgli. When in doubt, you can probably find me at a movie theater.
